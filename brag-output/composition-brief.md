# Hyperframes Composition Brief: AttendX Student Facial Scan Tutorial

## Objective
Create a polished, animated tutorial video demonstrating how students complete their 5-step biometric face scan calibration on the AttendX Student Portal.

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output/composition/`
- Render target: `brag-output/brag.mp4`
- Format: landscape (1280x720, 60fps)
- Duration: 24.0 seconds

## Source Material & Visuals
- Product: AttendX Student Biometric Portal
- Exact Portal URL: `https://one-attendx.vercel.app/student/login`
- Core Feature: 5 Guided Baseline Angles (Front, Left, Right, Chin Down, Smile)
- Color Palette: Dark mode (`#09090b`), Surface (`#121215`), Electric Blue (`#2563EB`), Emerald Green (`#10B981`), Cyan (`#06B6D4`)
- Typography: Inter + JetBrains Mono
