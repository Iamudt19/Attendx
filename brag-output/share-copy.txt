# AttendX Student Facial Scan Onboarding Guide (Share Copy)

🎥 Video Tutorial: Student Face ID Calibration in 5 Easy Steps
🔗 Portal Link: https://one-attendx.vercel.app/student/login

📌 Quick Summary:
1. Open the portal & click "Self-Enroll".
2. Fill your Student ID, Name, Roll No & Class, then create a Password.
3. Allow camera access.
4. Complete the 5 guided face angles (Front, Left, Right, Chin down, Smile).
5. Done! Your biometric profile is instantly active for automated 1.8s classroom attendance.
