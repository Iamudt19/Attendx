# Brag Plan: AttendX Student Facial Scan Guide (How-To Tutorial Video)

## What is this video?
A high-fidelity animated walkthrough showing students exactly how to complete their biometric face scan onboarding on the AttendX Student Portal in under 2 minutes.

## The Angle
"Zero Hassle, Instant Calibration." A sleek, modern product demo that walks students step-by-step through logging in, starting the camera, locking into the 5 guided facial angles (Front, Left, Right, Chin Down, Smile), and syncing their 128-D SFace biometric vector model for automated 1.8-second classroom attendance.

## Tone & Options
- Preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: Clean Swiss minimalist aesthetic with dark mode luxury accents, interactive 3D face mesh overlays, and crystal-clear step-by-step UI simulations.
- Format: `landscape` (16:9, 1280x720)
- Duration: 24.0s

## Storyboard
1. **Scene 1: Portal Access & Self-Enroll (0.0s – 4.5s)**
   - Display AttendX Student Portal header & Self-Enroll form.
   - Enter Student ID, Name, Cohort, and Password.
   - Callout: "Step 1: Self-Enroll on Student Portal".

2. **Scene 2: Camera Setup & Neural Mesh HUD (4.5s – 8.5s)**
   - Student clicks "Start Face Scan".
   - Live camera viewfinder opens with cybernetic YuNet bounding box & green neural face mesh overlay.
   - Callout: "Step 2: Position Face in Good Lighting".

3. **Scene 3: 5 Guided Baseline Angles Walkthrough (8.5s – 16.5s)**
   - Interactive sequence cycling through all 5 required angles with real-time feedback pills:
     • 😐 **Angle 1: Frontal Look** → Vector locked (✓)
     • ⬅️ **Angle 2: Left Turn** → Left profile saved (✓)
     • ➡️ **Angle 3: Right Turn** → Right profile saved (✓)
     • ⬇️ **Angle 4: Chin Down** → Depth vector saved (✓)
     • 😊 **Angle 5: Dynamic Smile** → Expression model saved (✓)
   - Real-time progress bar fills from 20% → 100%.

4. **Scene 4: Biometric Model Active & Analytics (16.5s – 20.5s)**
   - Confetti & celebration badge: "🎉 5/5 Face Angles Saved to Biometric Model".
   - Display student attendance percentage & live shortage simulator.
   - Callout: "Step 3: Automated 1.8-Second Roll-Call Activated".

5. **Scene 5: Summary & Portal Link Outro (20.5s – 24.0s)**
   - AttendX Brand Logo, key takeaway checklist, and portal URL: `one-attendx.vercel.app/student/login`.
